Privacy Policy

Mustard & Rye: A New York Deli Story

SupportPrivacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Mustard & Rye ("the game") is a single-player game about running a small pastrami deli.

We do not collect any data

The game has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send anything over the internet. It does not ask for the camera, microphone, location, contacts or any other permission. No personal information is collected from children or adults.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (level, savings, upgrades, stars) and settings (language, music, sound) are saved only on your own device. You can delete them at any time with New game on the main menu, or by deleting the game.

Steam

On Steam, the Steam client may collect information under Valve's own privacy policy. The game itself sends nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
