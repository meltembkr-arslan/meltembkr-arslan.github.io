How to play

Customers line up outside your little New York deli. To-go customers wait at the pickup counter; dine-in guests need a free, clean table. Tap an order ticket, stack the bread and fillings shown on the order card, then press Serve. Fast service earns bigger tips. Tap dirty tables to clear them.

Reach the day's goal to pass the level and earn up to 3 stars. New pastrami sandwiches unlock as you go, and from level 4 customers make special requests such as extra pastrami or no mustard. Spend your savings in the shop (the yellow deli icon at the top of the screen) on more tables, more pickup spots, a back room and helpful staff.

Frequently asked questions

Does the game need the internet?

No. It works fully offline.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts.

How do I change the language?

Tap English or Türkçe on the main menu, or in the in-game menu. Your choice is remembered.

How do I start over?

On the main menu, tap New game twice. This clears your levels, savings and upgrades on this device.

Keyboard shortcuts (Steam)

1–3 breads, Q–U fillings and sauces, Space or Enter to serve, Backspace to trash, Esc to pause, F11 for full screen.

My progress disappeared after reinstalling.

Progress is saved only on your device and is not uploaded anywhere, so it is removed when the game is deleted.
