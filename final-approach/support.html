Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Each chapter takes you to a part of the plane. Tap the items in the scene that match the list at the bottom. Some items are shown only by their shape. Pinch or scroll to zoom in. After each search you get a new piece of evidence for your case file.
I can't find the last item.
Tap the magnifier Hint button. It points to one item and then recharges over time. Zooming in also helps with small items.
How do I get 3 stars?
Finish the chapter quickly, with few misclicks and at most one hint. You can replay any chapter from the Cabin Map to improve your stars.
How do I solve the case?
Read the evidence and suspect notes in the Case File. At the deduction steps choose the answer that fits the evidence. If an answer does not fit, you can simply try again. Put all the evidence together to name the killer before the plane lands.
Where is my progress saved?
Only on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data resets your progress. You can also start over with New Case or Reset in Settings.
Can I play in Turkish?
Yes. Tap TR at the top of the screen or choose Türkçe in Settings. The game remembers your choice.
Is it suitable for children?
It is a murder mystery for ages 13 and up. A death by poisoning is part of the story, but nothing violent is shown.
Are there ads or purchases?
No. There are no ads and no in-app purchases, and the game works offline.
How do I turn off the music or sounds?
Open Settings on the title screen, or tap the menu button during a chapter. Music, effects and cabin ambience have separate volume controls.
