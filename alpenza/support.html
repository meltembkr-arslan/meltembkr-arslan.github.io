How to play

Tap a customer to see their order. Tap the dough, sauce, cheese and toppings on the order card, then press Bake. Take the pizza out of the oven when it glows green, then tap it on the pass to serve. Reach the day's coin goal to earn up to 3 stars, and spend your coins on upgrades to expand the shop and open new chapters.

Frequently asked questions

Does the game need the internet?

No, you can play offline. On iOS and Android an internet connection is only used to load ads.

Are there ads?

The iOS and Android versions show ads between some days, plus optional rewarded ads that double a day's coins. The Steam version has no ads.

How do I change the language?

Tap EN or TR on the main menu or at the top of the game screen.

How do I start over?

On the main menu, tap Reset progress twice.

Keyboard shortcuts (Steam)

B bake, T trash, 1 to 3 take a pizza out of an oven, Esc pause, F11 full screen.
