Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Pick a decoration in the bottom bar (Lights, Decor, Trees or Market), then tap a glowing spot in town to place it. Drag to move around and pinch or scroll to zoom. Settings › How to play explains everything step by step.
How do I open new districts?
Open the town map at the top left. Each district opens when you reach its level and the district before it is at least 50% festive. There are eight districts, the last one opens at level 30.
How do market stalls work?
Stalls earn coins on their own for up to 2 hours, even while the game is closed. Tap a stall to collect its coins or upgrade it.
Where is my progress saved?
Only on your device. Deleting the game or clearing its data resets your progress. Settings › Restart game starts over on purpose.
Can I play in Turkish?
Yes. The game opens in English; choose Türkçe in Settings (the gear at the top right).
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. Bellhaven has no ads and no in-app purchases.
Does the game need an internet connection?
No. You can play completely offline.
How do I change the music, sounds or church bells?
Open Settings (the gear at the top right) and use the Music, Sound effects and Church bells sliders.
