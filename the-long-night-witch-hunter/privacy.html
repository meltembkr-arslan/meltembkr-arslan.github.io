Privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026

The Long Night: Witch Hunter does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the game stores

  • Your progress, stars, gold, shop items and settings (language, volume, sensitivity) are saved only on your own device.
  • This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No internet connection is needed or used while you play.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
