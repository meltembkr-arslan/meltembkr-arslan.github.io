Frequently asked questions

How do I tell a witch from a person in costume?
Real witches cast no shadow, float slightly above the ground and give off a faint green glow. Hold LENS (right click or F on a computer) to reveal every witch within 50 metres.
How do the touch controls work?
Drag the stick at the bottom left to walk; push it all the way to run. Swipe an empty part of the screen to look around. Use ATTACK, LENS and JUMP on the right, and ⟳ to switch tools.
Why are some levels locked?
The 10 levels open one at a time. Finish a level to unlock the next one. Your best stars are kept for every level.
Where is my progress saved?
Only on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data resets your progress, gold and purchases from the in-game shop.
Can I play in Turkish?
Yes. The game opens in English; choose Turkish under Settings > Language.
Does the game need an internet connection?
No. It plays fully offline and has no ads and no account.
The game runs slowly on my phone.
Close other apps and restart the game. Phones use a lighter graphics setting automatically. If it is still slow, tell us your device model.
