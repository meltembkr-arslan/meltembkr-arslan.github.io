Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Drive your fire engine to the scene, spray water on the flames and rescue anyone trapped before the timer runs out. Fires you leave alone grow and spread, so choose which blaze to fight first.
How do I unlock new districts?
There are 40 missions in 5 districts. Finish missions and earn stars to open the next ones. Every district ends with a boss mission.
What are coins for?
Spend them in the garage to upgrade your fire engine, such as a bigger tank, a stronger hose or a faster truck.
Where is my progress saved?
Only on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data resets your progress.
Can I play in Turkish?
Yes. The game opens in English; choose Türkçe on the main menu or in the in-game menu.
Are there ads or purchases?
There are no in-app purchases. The free Android and iOS versions show ads: optional videos you can watch for extra coins, and from mission 4 onwards a short ad after every third mission, never during play. The Steam version has no ads and works offline.
How do I turn off the music?
Tap the music button, which is always visible during a mission and on the main menu.
