Privacy Policy

North's Boxes: Shape Matching

SupportPrivacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

North's Boxes: Shape Matching ("the game") is made for children and their families.

We do not collect any data

The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use the internet. It does not ask for the camera, microphone, location, contacts or any other permission. No personal information is collected from children or adults.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (levels, stars, achievements and settings) is saved only on your own device. You can delete it at any time with Settings › Reset Progress, or by deleting the game.

Steam

On Steam, achievements are reported to Steam by the Steam client, under Valve's own privacy policy. The game itself sends nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
