How to play

Each level opens a toy box full of shape shadows. Drag a shape from the shelf and drop it on its matching shadow. Fill every cell before the time runs out to finish the level and earn up to 3 stars.

From level 11, the color has to match too. There are 20 levels, 18 shapes and 16 achievements.

Frequently asked questions

Does the game need the internet?

No. It works fully offline.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts.

How do I change the music or the language?

Open Settings from the main menu. You can switch between English and Turkish, pick one of four songs, and turn music or sound effects on and off.

How do I start over?

Open Settings and tap Reset Progress twice. This clears all levels, stars and achievements on this device.

My progress disappeared after reinstalling.

Progress is saved only on your device and is not uploaded anywhere, so it is removed when the game is deleted.
