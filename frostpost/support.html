Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Your snowmobile drives on its own. At each junction, tap an arrow to choose the next road (on a computer: click it or use the arrow keys). Hold anywhere on the screen, or Space, to brake. Deliver every parcel before the timer runs out.
Why does my snowmobile spin out?
Icy curves are slippery. When a yellow or red ring appears around the sled, brake before the curve. Ice Skis in the Garage give you more grip.
What are the orange parcels and the mountain passes?
Orange parcels are Express: they have their own countdown. Mountain passes are shortcuts that close at the time shown on their sign.
How do I unlock new regions?
Each level gives up to three stars. New regions open when you have collected enough stars: 8 for Glasslake, 20 for Ironridge Pass and 34 for Whiteout Peaks.
Where is my progress saved?
Only on your device. Deleting the game or clearing its data resets your progress. Settings › Reset Progress starts over on purpose.
Can I play in Turkish?
Yes. The game opens in English; tap TR in the language switch at the top right of any screen.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. There are no ads and no in-app purchases, and the game works without an internet connection.
