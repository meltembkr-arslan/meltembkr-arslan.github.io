Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Pick a cake shape and number of tiers, choose a frosting color, add a drizzle and a border, then pick toppings and tap the cake to place them. Spin the cake by dragging it. When you are happy, press the finish button to celebrate.
Why are some items locked?
New shapes, colors, drizzles, borders and toppings unlock as you play: every 3 cakes you decorate opens a new level. A cake counts when it has at least 3 toppings.
Where is my progress saved?
Only on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data resets your progress.
Can I play in Turkish?
Yes. The game opens in English; tap the EN button at the top to switch to Turkish.
Are there ads?
The phone and tablet versions show a short ad between some cakes. All ads are set as child-directed, are never personalized and are rated for general audiences. The Steam version has no ads.
Does the game need an internet connection?
No. You can play offline. On phones and tablets ads only appear when you are online.
How do I turn off the music or sounds?
Use the music note and speaker buttons at the top right of the screen.
