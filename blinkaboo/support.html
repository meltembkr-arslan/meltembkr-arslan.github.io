Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Ghosts blink in and out of sight. Tap or click a ghost while you can see it to catch it. Catch the number of ghosts shown at the top before the timer runs out. Faint sparkles show where an invisible ghost is floating.
How do I get 3 stars?
Finish the level, keep at least 30% of the time and hit at least 75% of your taps. Tapping empty space or the sleepy cloud lowers your accuracy.
How do I open the next world?
Beat the Grand Boo at the end of the world and collect enough stars. The map shows how many stars you need. Endless mode opens after World 1.
What do the power-ups do?
The lantern makes every ghost visible for a few seconds, frost slows the ghosts down and the big net makes catching easier. Tap a glowing orb to use it. Golden ghosts always leave one behind.
Where is my progress saved?
Only on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data resets your progress.
Can I play in Turkish?
Yes. The game opens in English; tap TR on the title screen or choose Türkçe in Settings. The game starts in English each time it opens.
Are there ads or purchases?
No. There are no ads and no in-app purchases, and the game works offline.
How do I turn off the music or sounds?
Open Settings on the title screen, or press pause during a level.
