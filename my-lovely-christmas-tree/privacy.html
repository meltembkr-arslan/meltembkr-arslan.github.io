Privacy Policy

My Lovely Christmas Tree · Effective date: 1 November 2026

My Lovely Christmas Tree ("the game") is developed and published by an independent developer ("we"). We respect your privacy. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play the game on Steam, iOS, iPadOS or Android.

We do not collect personal data

The game does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking technologies and no third-party SDKs that collect data. It does not require an account and works fully offline.

Data stored on your device

Your decorated trees, unlocked levels and settings (language, sound, graphics quality) are saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Platform providers

Valve (Steam), Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) may process data related to your purchase and download under their own privacy policies. We do not receive personal information from them beyond anonymous, aggregated sales statistics.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
